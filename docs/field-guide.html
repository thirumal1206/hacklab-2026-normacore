Alfred: an LLM that runs a robot arm by looking through its cameras

The model decides what to do and checks whether it worked. Ordinary code measures, plans the joint angles and keeps the arm safe. Everything below was built and measured in a MuJoCo simulation; the real arm is still untested.

Plate 2 · Sequence

"Alfred, put the red cube in the box"

One spoken request, end to end. Alfred narrates nothing; she says one line when the camera has confirmed the result.

Plate 3 · Sequence

Talking over her: "Alfred, stop"

Hearing the wake word cuts her voice at once. A stop word freezes the arm in the middle of a move, and nothing moves again until the model has listened.

Plate 4 · Pipeline

How a pick works

The model only chooses the target id. Every other step is code, and each step either succeeds or reports why, so the model can retry or ask.

choose · model

Pick a numbered mark

  • locate lists objects with id, shape, position, container
  • Model picks red; never raw pixels
see · code

Find it precisely

  • Colour masks + empty-table difference
  • Homography → table cm
  • snap recentres and reads the yaw
plan · code

Solve the joints

  • IK, gripper pointing down
  • Several seed postures
  • Arrive from above, lower travel height if needed
grasp · code

Close and lift

  • Descend to 1.2 cm
  • Real arm: close in steps, stop when the jaws stop
  • Lift in 3 short steps
  • Missed? rotate jaws 90° and retry once
verify · model

Look again

  • place box drops in a free corner
  • locate confirms "inside"
  • Only then: "Done."
Plate 5 · Defence in depth

Five rings between a sentence and a servo

The outer rings guide the model; the inner rings hold even if the model is wrong. Nothing safety-critical depends on the prompt.

Operator rules. One physical command per step, re-locate before each pick, refuse throwing or moving toward people, "Done" only after the camera confirms.

MCP tool list. Only safe verbs exist. No score, reset or teleport tools; nothing takes joint angles from the model.

Daemon gate. Workspace box, 0.8 cm z floor, IK must reach the target, stop words freeze motion, one arm command at a time on one thread.

Station backend. Follower bus pinned, uncalibrated motors refused, speed 250 / accel 10, goals clamped to calibrated range, stall and overload hold position, stale camera feed rejected.

relax. Torque off on every motor: the emergency stop, with a hand on the arm during first runs.

Plate 8 · Status

Where we stand before Saturday

Works in simulation measured

  • Locate, pick, place, recover from a knocked-out object
  • Non-cube objects: pen, cup, eraser, tape
  • Alfred: hands-free, interruptible, refuses unsafe asks
  • MCP and CLI; Haiku at about $0.02 per task
  • Station client, VP8 frames, bus and calibration guards

Built, not proven unverified

  • ElRobot joint directions and gripper polarity
  • Camera calibration error on the printed sheet
  • Stall and overload hold on real servos
  • Echo filter with laptop speakers
  • Station on a Raspberry Pi over SSH

Known gaps open

  • Flat objects like a pencil lying down
  • Picking from a crowded box disturbs neighbours
  • Collisions with the table are not modelled
  • No click-to-teach or camera auto-rebind yet
